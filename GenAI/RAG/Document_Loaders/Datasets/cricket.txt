
Cricket is not just a game,
It is a passion, pride, and fame.
When the bat strikes the ball so high,
Hope and dreams reach the sky.

Every boundary brings a smile,
Every wicket makes us wait a while.
Players fight with strength and pride,
With teamwork always by their side.

Victory brings joy to every heart,
While every loss teaches a brand-new start.
On the field, courage always shines,
Through hard work and challenging times.

Every match creates memories bright,
Every moment feels exciting and right.
Cricket unites us, near and far,
And makes every player a shining star.
